The Uday Accord was signed eight months after the National Energy Bureau hearing, formally ending the suspension of Project Uday under a new governance framework. Suryodaya Energy Group, Konkan Consortium, and the Central Renewable Council were all signatories, alongside representatives of the Kutch Solar Initiative.

Under The Uday Accord, oversight of future battery installations passed to an expanded Kutch Solar Initiative, a body reorganized to review safety data independently of Suryodaya Energy Group. The Kutch Solar Initiative is part of the Central Renewable Council, but operates with its own dedicated engineering staff and reporting lines in Ahmedabad.

Vikram Sinha stepped down as chief operating officer of Suryodaya Energy Group shortly before The Uday Accord was finalized, and Priya Nair left her legal role soon after to join the Kutch Solar Initiative as a compliance advisor. Dr. Ananya Rao, who had testified before the National Energy Bureau, was invited to join the Kutch Solar Initiative's advisory board.

Konkan Consortium welcomed the creation of the expanded Kutch Solar Initiative but said it would continue monitoring Suryodaya Energy Group closely. Deccan Wire Press, through continued reporting by Arjun Mehta, remained the primary outlet tracking implementation of The Uday Accord in the months that followed.
