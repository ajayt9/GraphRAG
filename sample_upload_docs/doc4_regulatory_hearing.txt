The National Energy Bureau convened a formal hearing in Delhi to examine the causes of The Battery Incident and the broader conduct of Project Uday. Representatives from Suryodaya Energy Group, Konkan Consortium, and the Central Renewable Council were called to testify before a panel of regulators and independent engineers.

Vikram Sinha testified first, defending the safety record of Suryodaya Energy Group and arguing that The Battery Incident resulted from an isolated equipment failure rather than any systemic flaw in the project's design. Priya Nair appeared alongside him, addressing questions about the agreements between Suryodaya Energy Group and Konkan Consortium.

Dr. Ananya Rao also testified before the National Energy Bureau, presenting the internal memos she had shared with Deccan Wire Press. Her testimony directly contradicted several claims made by Vikram Sinha, prompting sharp questioning from panel members representing the Central Renewable Council.

Arjun Mehta, a senior reporter for Deccan Wire Press, covered the hearing extensively, noting that Konkan Consortium had submitted its own independent data to the National Energy Bureau. The Bureau ultimately ordered Suryodaya Energy Group to halt all further battery installations until a full third-party audit could be completed, a decision the Central Renewable Council endorsed shortly after the hearing concluded.
