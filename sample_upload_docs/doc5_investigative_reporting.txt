Arjun Mehta spent months investigating the financial ties behind Project Uday for Deccan Wire Press, tracing contracts and subsidiary filings that connected Konkan Consortium to a wider network of renewable energy investors. His reporting traced payments from Konkan Consortium through several intermediaries before reaching Suryodaya Energy Group directly.

The investigation also examined the Kutch Solar Initiative, a government-backed program that Arjun Mehta discovered was closely tied to Konkan Consortium funding. The Kutch Solar Initiative had quietly backed several battery-storage ventures across Gujarat, of which Project Uday was by far the largest and most controversial.

Arjun Mehta reported that Konkan Consortium had lobbied the Central Renewable Council to relax certification requirements for battery installations, a claim the Council denied. Deccan Wire Press published internal correspondence appearing to show representatives of Konkan Consortium meeting privately with Council staff in Delhi before key certification decisions.

Dr. Ananya Rao praised the reporting by Arjun Mehta, saying it confirmed suspicions she had raised since her own testimony before the National Energy Bureau. Suryodaya Energy Group, reached for comment, said Konkan Consortium's involvement helped explain the commercial pressure described throughout the investigation.
