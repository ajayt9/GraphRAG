Dr. Ananya Rao agreed to speak publicly about Project Uday six weeks after the Central Renewable Council suspended operations. In a lengthy interview, she described how Suryodaya Energy Group had downplayed early warning signs long before The Battery Incident brought the program under scrutiny.

According to Dr. Ananya Rao, she joined Suryodaya Energy Group as a senior researcher and worked directly with Vikram Sinha to design the original battery safety protocols. Over time, she said, commercial pressure from Konkan Consortium investors pushed the team toward faster deployment schedules than the engineering supported.

Dr. Ananya Rao shared internal safety memos with Deccan Wire Press, disclosing that Suryodaya Energy Group had detected thermal anomalies in the battery modules months before the incident. She said she had raised concerns with Priya Nair, who advised that the findings be reported to the Central Renewable Council, advice that was reportedly overruled by senior leadership.

The disclosures shared by Dr. Ananya Rao prompted Konkan Consortium to expand its own independent investigation, and Deccan Wire Press published a series of follow-up stories examining the relationship between Suryodaya Energy Group and its investors. Dr. Ananya Rao ultimately resigned from Suryodaya Energy Group, citing an inability to continue working under what she called a culture of concealment.
