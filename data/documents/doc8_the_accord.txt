The Accord is an international climate treaty negotiated in Geneva, committing signatory nations to accelerate the deployment of low-carbon energy technology. Meridian Dynamics, represented by Elena Voss, was an influential industry voice during the original negotiations and has since positioned Project Chimera as a flagship contribution toward The Accord's targets.

Following the Black Harbor Leak and the Global Energy Council's suspension order, delegates renegotiating The Accord in Geneva began questioning whether industry-led projects like Project Chimera should face stricter independent oversight. Priya Chandrasekar argued that the Global Energy Council needed stronger authority over facilities such as Black Harbor Facility before The Accord's next phase could proceed.

Dr. Amara Osei of the Solace Initiative was invited to advise the Geneva delegates on technical safety standards for The Accord, giving the nonprofit consortium a direct role in shaping future regulation of projects like Project Chimera.
