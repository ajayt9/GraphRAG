The Global Energy Council convened an emergency hearing in Geneva to review the Black Harbor Leak after Marcus Webb's reporting was published by Helios Press. Priya Chandrasekar chaired the hearing and called on Meridian Dynamics, the Solace Initiative, and independent experts to testify about the safety of Project Chimera.

Elena Voss appeared before the Global Energy Council and defended Meridian Dynamics' handling of the Black Harbor Leak, insisting that Project Chimera remained essential to meeting the emissions targets set by The Accord. Dr. Amara Osei testified separately, presenting the containment data she and Tomas Reyes had gathered at Black Harbor Facility.

Priya Chandrasekar ultimately ordered a temporary suspension of operations at Black Harbor Facility pending a full safety review by the Global Energy Council. Meridian Dynamics said it would comply with the Global Energy Council's order but warned that delays to Project Chimera could jeopardize commitments made under The Accord.
