The Solace Initiative is an independent nonprofit research consortium founded to provide public oversight of large-scale energy experiments. Dr. Amara Osei, a plasma physicist, leads the Solace Initiative and has spent the last decade studying fusion containment safety. She was invited to Black Harbor Facility as an independent auditor after Meridian Dynamics agreed, under pressure from the Global Energy Council, to allow external review of Project Chimera.

Dr. Amara Osei quickly grew concerned about safety shortcuts at Black Harbor Facility. She documented irregular containment readings and raised them with Tomas Reyes, a senior engineer on Project Chimera who shared her concerns. Together, Dr. Amara Osei and Tomas Reyes began compiling evidence that Meridian Dynamics was suppressing safety warnings to keep Project Chimera on schedule.

The Solace Initiative has close ties to the Global Energy Council, the regulatory body responsible for certifying experimental energy facilities across Europe. Priya Chandrasekar, the lead regulator at the Global Energy Council, has relied on reports from the Solace Initiative when deciding whether to approve continued operation of Black Harbor Facility.
