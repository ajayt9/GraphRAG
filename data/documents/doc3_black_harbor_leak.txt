Six weeks ago, a containment seal failure at the Black Harbor Facility released a small amount of radioactive coolant into the surrounding fjord. Meridian Dynamics initially described the event, now widely known as the Black Harbor Leak, as a minor maintenance issue with no safety implications. Tomas Reyes, who was on shift during the failure, later said the leak was far more serious than the company admitted.

Tomas Reyes decided to become a whistleblower after Meridian Dynamics ordered him to alter his incident report on the Black Harbor Leak. He secretly copied internal documents about Project Chimera and shared them with Dr. Amara Osei of the Solace Initiative, who helped him find a safe way to make the information public.

Viktor Kessler and the Obsidian Group were assigned to investigate the source of the leaked documents. Kessler traced early digital traces back to Tomas Reyes and flagged him internally as a security risk, though Meridian Dynamics had not yet taken any public action against him at the time.
