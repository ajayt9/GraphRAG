Marcus Webb is an investigative journalist at Helios Press, a news outlet based in Singapore that focuses on energy and climate reporting. Marcus Webb first heard about irregularities at Black Harbor Facility from a source close to the Solace Initiative and began digging into Meridian Dynamics and Project Chimera.

After Tomas Reyes leaked internal documents describing the Black Harbor Leak, Marcus Webb obtained copies through Dr. Amara Osei and spent two months verifying their authenticity. Marcus Webb also reached out to Priya Chandrasekar at the Global Energy Council to ask why regulators had not disclosed the incident publicly.

Marcus Webb's reporting for Helios Press ultimately named Elena Voss directly, alleging that she had personally approved the decision to downplay the Black Harbor Leak. Meridian Dynamics denied the allegations and suggested that Helios Press was being used to damage the company ahead of renewed negotiations over The Accord.
