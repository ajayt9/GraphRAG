Tomas Reyes joined Meridian Dynamics as an engineer six years ago and was transferred to Project Chimera shortly after it began at Black Harbor Facility. He said he initially trusted the project's safety culture, but that trust eroded after he raised concerns about containment readings and was told by supervisors to keep the issue out of formal reports.

In an interview arranged by Marcus Webb, Tomas Reyes described how Dr. Amara Osei's arrival as an independent auditor from the Solace Initiative gave him the confidence to document what he was seeing. Tomas Reyes said he shared his findings with Dr. Amara Osei before the Black Harbor Leak occurred, not after.

Tomas Reyes has since left Meridian Dynamics and says he is cooperating with the Global Energy Council's safety review. He has not ruled out further legal exposure, noting that Viktor Kessler and the Obsidian Group had already identified him as the likely source of the leaked Project Chimera documents.
