The Obsidian Group has worked for Meridian Dynamics since before Project Chimera began, providing security not only at Black Harbor Facility but also at Meridian Dynamics offices in Geneva and Lagos. Viktor Kessler joined the Obsidian Group after leaving government intelligence work and quickly became the firm's point of contact for Meridian Dynamics.

After the Black Harbor Leak became public, Viktor Kessler was asked by Elena Voss to determine how Tomas Reyes had obtained internal Project Chimera documents. The Obsidian Group's internal investigation concluded that Tomas Reyes had worked with Dr. Amara Osei and, indirectly, with Marcus Webb of Helios Press to disclose the material.

Viktor Kessler recommended that Meridian Dynamics pursue legal action against Tomas Reyes, but Elena Voss hesitated, wary of drawing further attention from the Global Energy Council and from Priya Chandrasekar in particular during an already sensitive period for The Accord negotiations.
